## Conclusion

The rules have no edge on stocks that were not hand-picked: about 0R a trade. Their good results on the
accounts' list come from the list itself (NVDA, AMD, AVGO, META were picked knowing they had become
winners). The same finding as the finder's audit of the live strategies (random entries with the same
exits did as well). Pushing these rules harder (account E) multiplies a zero edge: not done.
E waits for an edge that passes the finder; until then it stays flat.
