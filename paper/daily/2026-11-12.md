## A night of false alerts, and the fix

- **What happened:** from the close on, the check that compares Alpaca with the plan reported "STC: 0.023106
  held, only 0 covered by a stop" at every run, and sent the phone a "[A] Alpaca does not match the plan"
  alert each time.
  - **At least 17 alerts**, one per run from 21:20 UTC to 03:50 UTC. Possibly up to 26, if it started when the
    stop filled at 17:25 UTC; I could not confirm the earlier ones.
  - Nothing was wrong with the account. Every real position had its stop.
- **Cause:** a bug in the check. Once STC had left the plan, the check had no price for it and used a default
  of $100, so 96 cents looked like $2.31, above the $1 line under which a rest needs no stop.
- **Why it lasted the night:** I found the bug at 18:20 UTC, wrote the test and the fix, and the session stopped
  before I pushed them. It was pushed at 03:57 UTC on Friday.
- **The fix** (checks and alerts only; no order changes):
  - a holding the plan has no price for is priced at the market;
  - a rest of a position the plan no longer wants is dust under $5, the size the sync never trades anyway.
  - Test first: it fails on the old code. A position the plan wants without its stop is still reported, and so
    is a rest worth more than $5.
