  - Its opening auction printed 62.20 at 14:30:00 UTC (682 shares). A's order was not in it: it was a plain market
    order queued at 09:23 UTC.

    price, and in the backtest the rule earns about +0.14 to +0.20R a trade. -0.06R a buy is a third of that.
    A limit order near the opening price for small, thinly traded stocks is a question for after the freeze.
