## Why the real fills differ from the paper (research today)

`reports/2026-10-20-entry-timing/README.md`: the orders go out about 80 minutes after the paper's entry price,
not 20, because the paper books an entry only once the bar after the signal is complete. On 12,482 past signals,
the price 5-120 minutes later is the same on average (0.00R), and limit orders below the price are never better
(they miss the winners). The 40 entries' gap is market luck, not "buying too early". A fix that sends the order
one hour earlier (and overnight signals to the 9:30 auction) waits for the user's choice: now or after 6 Dec.

## Bug fixed tonight: stop check after the open

The check that keeps a stopped-out trade from being bought back looks at the prices since the paper's last bar.
That "last bar" came from equity.csv, which already has the hour that just ended as soon as the crypto bar is
final, while the stock bar of that hour is final only 15 minutes later. At 14:01 UTC the engine (shadow) started
its range at 14:00, missed AAPL's stop at the 13:30 open, and **would have bought AAPL back** in A and C (nothing
was sent: shadow). The hourly system was exposed only to runs starting between :00 and :15 (GitHub's delayed backup
runs sometimes do); none did today. Fix: the paper now writes each position's last processed bar into orders.json
and the check starts after that bar, per stock. Tests added (2), all 187 tests pass; the accounts' decisions do not
change (only the stop check of algo.alpaca).
