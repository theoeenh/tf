## Fixed today (bug fixes, tests first)

1. **Pre-open runs re-sent the queued orders again.** Before the open Alpaca checks a sell stop against its
   reference price; for STC that was 30.69 all morning (it closed at 43.48), so its 37.19 stop was refused and the
   fallback put it just under (30.68). The check that leaves correct queued orders alone then never matched the
   plan, so every run from 6:53 to 9:23 cancelled and re-sent A's FFIN buy and SPY sale (7 times). The stand-in
   stop now records the planned level it replaces and counts as it while the market is closed (morning fix).
2. **The stand-in is tried again at the planned level.** The constant re-sending used to retry the planned stop
   too (Tuesday's BBT came back by 18:50). Runs that leave the queue alone now try each stand-in again at its
   planned level, replacing it only if Alpaca accepts it. Tonight BBT (26.54 for 28.89) and FFIN (23.08 for
   28.28) are stand-ins: Alpaca's after-hours reference for these thin stocks is far below their close.
3. **Finder (research code):** the parameter tweak could push a share of the universe above 100% (yesterday's
   insider_stress_low_short with max_rank 0.98 → 1.37), which switches the filter off: its "13/14" was
   insider_sector_stress counted again. Shares now stay below 1 (5 of 4,918 strategies were affected).
